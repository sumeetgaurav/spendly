Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 7, 2026

1. Data We Collect

To provide the Spendly service, we collect the information you give us directly, such as your name, email address, and password when you create an account. We also store the expense data you enter, including amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions.

  • Account information: name, email address, and password (stored securely).
  • Expense records: amounts, categories, dates, and any notes you add.
  • Basic usage information, such as login timestamps, needed to keep your account secure.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use the information you provide solely to operate and improve Spendly. This includes displaying your expense history, generating summaries and category breakdowns, and authenticating your account when you sign in.

We do not use your financial data for advertising, and we do not sell your personal information to third parties.

3. Data Storage

Your account and expense data are stored in our database and retained for as long as your account remains active. We take reasonable technical measures to protect your data from unauthorized access, loss, or misuse.

You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time. Once deleted, your data cannot be recovered.

4. Third-Party Services

Spendly may rely on third-party services for functionality such as hosting, authentication, or font delivery (for example, Google Fonts). These providers only receive the minimum information necessary to perform their function and are not permitted to use your data for their own purposes.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please reach out to us through the support channel listed in your account settings, and we'll be glad to help.
